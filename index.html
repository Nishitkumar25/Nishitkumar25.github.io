NoshGuard: Smart Product Advisor

AI-powered advisor that reads packaged-food ingredient lists from a photo, evaluates them against a personal dietary profile, and returns an explainable decision with safer alternatives.

problem
Ingredient lists are dense, unfamiliar, and rarely matched to the reader's own dietary constraints. Generic food sites ignore personal allergens, conditions, or dietary choices, and few tools offer an explanation for why a product is (or isn't) suitable.

aispeech-processingresearch

Adaptive Emotion-Aware Speech Therapy Framework for Dysarthria

An AI-based research framework for adaptive speech therapy. It analyses a speaker's speech characteristics, emotional state, accent variation, and severity level then generates therapy exercises that adapt to the speaker rather than applying one fixed routine.

problem
Dysarthria affects speech clarity and intelligibility, usually with individualized impairments. Conventional therapy routines are generic and rarely account for the speaker's current emotional state, accent, or severity reducing both engagement and effectiveness.
approach
A speech-driven loop: analyze the patient's input, estimate characteristics and severity, detect emotion and accent influence, then adapt therapy content and difficulty. Feedback closes the loop and re-adjusts the next session.

Research

Adaptive Emotion-Aware Speech Therapy Framework for Dysarthria Using AI

problem
Conventional dysarthria therapy applies fixed exercise routines, ignoring each speaker's unique impairment profile severity, accent, and emotional state during a session.
motivation
Therapy is more effective when it adapts. A framework that continuously reads the speaker's speech and emotion signals can keep exercises challenging but achievable, and adjust when the speaker is struggling.
methodology
Speech input is preprocessed and reduced to acoustic features. Models classify speech characteristics, emotion, and severity; the assessment feeds an adaptive therapy generator that selects and stages exercises; feedback updates the next round.
ai / ml approach
Machine and deep learning classifiers over extracted speech features for emotion and severity estimation, with accent-aware feature handling, and a rule-informed therapy decision layer that converts model output into staged exercise plans.
dataset context
Developed as a research framework, evaluation targets standard dysarthric and emotion speech corpora in the domain.
contribution
Defined the adaptive therapy decision layer that connects speech/emotion analysis to severity-appropriate exercise generation turning a one-size-fits-all therapy model into a measurable, feedback-driven system.
