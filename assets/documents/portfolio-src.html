/04 /

Research

Adaptive Emotion-Aware Speech Therapy Framework for Dysarthria Using AI

research_problem

Conventional dysarthria therapy applies fixed exercise routines, ignoring each speaker's unique impairment profile severity, accent variation, and emotional state during a session.

motivation

Therapy is more effective when it adapts. Continuously reading speech and emotion signals lets exercises stay challenging but achievable, and adjust when the speaker is struggling.

methodology

Input speech is preprocessed and reduced to acoustic features. Models classify speech characteristics, emotion, and severity; the assessment feeds an adaptive therapy generator that stages exercises; feedback updates the next round.

ai / ml approach

Machine and deep learning classifiers over extracted speech features for emotion and severity estimation, accent-aware feature handling, and a therapy decision layer that turns model output into staged exercise plans.

speech_therapy_loop

Input Speech→ Preprocessing→ Feature Extraction→ Speech / Emotion Analysis→ Dysarthria Assessment→ Adaptive Therapy→ Feedback
contribution
Defined the adaptive therapy decision layer that connects speech and emotion analysis to severity-appropriate exercise generation turning a one-size-fits-all therapy model into a measurable, feedback-driven system.
dataset context
Developed as a research framework; evaluation targets standard dysarthric and emotion speech corpora in the domain.
